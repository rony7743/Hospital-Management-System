## 1. 🩺 Doctor Appointments
- **Department/Specialty Listing:** Browse and select departments such as Cardiology, Medicine, Neurology, Orthopedics, etc.
- **Doctor Profiles:** View doctor photos, educational qualifications, experience, visiting fees, and chamber schedules.
- **Slot Booking Calendar:** Select a preferred date and time slot and book an appointment.
- **Payment & Confirmation:** Pay online via bKash, Nagad, or Card, or choose to pay at the hospital. Download the appointment/serial slip after confirmation.

## 2. 🔬 Lab & Diagnostics
- **Test Catalog:** Browse available tests such as CBC, Lipid Profile, X-Ray, MRI, USG, etc., along with their prices.
- **Test Booking:** Schedule a time for sample collection at the lab or request home sample collection.
- **Online Report Viewer:** Receive notifications when reports are ready and view or download PDF reports directly from the platform.

## 3. 🛏️ Wards & ICU Beds
- **Real-Time Bed Availability:** Check available beds in General Wards, CCU, ICU, VIP Cabins, etc.
- **Room & Facility Details:** View facilities such as AC/Non-AC, refrigerator, TV, etc., along with daily charges.
- **Emergency Admission Request:** Submit patient information and request or hold a bed for emergency admission.

## 4. 💊 24/7 Pharmacy
- **Medicine Search & Cart:** Search medicines by brand or generic name and add them to the shopping cart.
- **Prescription Upload:** Upload a photo of a prescription so that a pharmacist can review it and convert it into a medicine order.
- **Home Delivery Tracking:** Track the delivery status of ordered medicines in real time.

## 5. 📹 Teleconsultation
- **Online Doctor Listing:** See which doctors are currently available for online consultation.
- **Video Consultation System:** Conduct one-to-one video/audio consultations using WebRTC or video SDKs such as Agora, Twilio, or Daily.co.
- **Digital Prescription:** After the consultation, the doctor can issue a digital prescription that becomes available directly in the patient's account.

## 6. 🩸 Blood Bank
- **Blood Stock Information:** View the available quantity of different blood groups such as A+, B+, O+, AB-, etc.
- **Donor Search:** Search for voluntary blood donors by blood group and location, with options to contact them.
- **Donor Registration:** Allow users to register as voluntary blood donors by providing their name, phone number, blood group, and other necessary information.

## 7. 🏠 Home Healthcare
- **Service Selection:** Choose services such as nursing care, injection/saline administration, wound dressing, oxygen cylinder assistance, physiotherapy, etc.
- **Schedule & Address Booking:** Select the preferred date, time, and location where the healthcare professional should visit.
- **Caregiver Tracking:** View the assigned nurse/caregiver's profile and track their arrival/service status.

## 8. 📑 Medical Records
- **Prescription History:** Store and organize all previous prescriptions by date and doctor.
- **Document Upload:** Scan and upload previous medical reports, prescriptions, and other documents using a phone camera.
